PinCut

PinCut is a Mac screenshot and screen recording utility built for everyday visual work. Capture, pin, annotate, scroll, OCR, QR, record, and export GIFs in one local-first workflow.

Product Highlights



Precise Capture


Capture areas, windows, full screens, and long scrolling pages with fast controls designed for macOS.




Floating Pins


Pin screenshots, clipboard images, files, text, and colors as floating references while you keep working.




Annotation Tools


Add arrows, text, blur, crop, mockups, and redaction directly after capture.




OCR, QR, Recording, and GIF


Extract text, detect QR codes, record the screen, trim video, and export GIFs without leaving the app.

Privacy

PinCut is designed as a local-first Mac utility. Captures, pins, OCR/QR results, history, recordings, and GIF exports stay on your Mac unless you choose to share or export them. Read the full PinCut Privacy Policy.
