PinCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: Jun 20, 2026

PinCut is a local-first Mac screenshot, pinboard, annotation, OCR, QR, screen recording, and GIF utility. This policy explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

  • Screen content you capture: Screenshots, scrolling captures, pinned images, recordings, and GIF exports are created only when you use the app’s capture or recording features.
  • Local history and preferences: PinCut may store recent capture history, tool settings, shortcuts, annotation preferences, and quick panel items locally on your Mac.
  • OCR and QR results: Text extracted by OCR and QR code recognition results are processed for the feature you request and are not sent to our servers.
  • Crash diagnostics: If you choose to share diagnostics with developers in macOS settings, Apple may provide anonymized crash logs that help us fix bugs.

How We Use the Data

  1. Provide capture, pinning, annotation, OCR, QR, recording, GIF export, and history features.
  2. Remember your local preferences and recent workflow state.
  3. Diagnose crashes and improve app stability when anonymized diagnostics are provided by Apple.

Data Storage and Sharing

PinCut does not operate a separate cloud service for your captures. Your screenshots, pinned items, OCR/QR results, recording files, GIFs, and history stay on your Mac unless you choose to copy, export, share, or save them to a location synchronized by another service.

We do not sell your data, share your content with advertisers, or use third-party analytics SDKs to track your activity across apps or websites.

macOS Permissions

  • Screen Recording: Required for screenshot and screen recording workflows.
  • Microphone: Optional, used only when you choose to record audio.
  • Files and folders: Used when you choose files to pin, save, export, or open.
  • Accessibility: May be requested for workflows that need to observe or display keyboard and mouse interactions.

Children’s Privacy

PinCut is a productivity utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

  • You can remove local PinCut data by deleting the app’s local history and preferences, or by deleting the app and its local containers from your Mac.
  • You can revoke macOS permissions at any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • You can opt out of diagnostics sharing in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact us at liupeng.skstore@gmail.com.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy as PinCut evolves. The latest version will always be available at https://liufsd.github.io/blog/pincut/privacy/.
