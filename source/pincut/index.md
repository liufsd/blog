## Privacy

PinCut is designed as a local-first Mac utility. Captures, pins, OCR/QR results, history, recordings, and GIF exports stay on your Mac unless you choose to share or export them. Read the full [PinCut Privacy Policy](/blog/pincut/privacy/).
